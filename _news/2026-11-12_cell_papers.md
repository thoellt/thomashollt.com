Together with our collaborators from the [BRAIN Initiative Cell Atlas Network (BICAN)](https://brain-bican.org/science/basal-ganglia), we just published two papers on the [primate basal ganglia](/publications/2026_basalganglia/) and the [primate subcortex](/publications/2026_primatesubcortex/) as part of the [Cell special issue](https://www.cell.com/consortium/bican-basal-ganglia-atlas) presenting the basal ganglia atlas. 
The atlas has been picked up by several media outlets, including [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/brain-cell-map-basal-ganglia-neuron-schizophrenia) and [El País](https://english.elpais.com/science-tech/2026-11-12/scientists-create-largest-ever-map-of-brain-region-tied-to-parkinsons-alzheimers-schizophrenia-and-autism.html). Big congratulations and thanks to the whole [Cytosplore Viewer](https://viewer.cytosplore.org) team for the hard work.
