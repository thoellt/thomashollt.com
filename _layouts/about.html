Hello, I am Thomas.

I am a professor for visualization in the Vis Group at the University of Bergen, Norway.

I build visual analytics tools that help people make sense of large, high-dimensional data, usually together with biologists and medical researchers. Right now I am working on dimensionality reduction, on tools for exploring single-cell and spatial omics data, and on how interactive visualization and AI can work hand in hand.

Looking for a master thesis? I have open projects from fundamental to very applied, so if any of this sounds interesting, get in touch and we can find something that fits you.
