    <h2><span class="dab c-sage"></span>Bio</h2>
    <p id="bio">Thomas Höllt is a professor of visualization in the Vis Group at the University of Bergen, Norway. He received his PhD in computer science from King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in 2013. He then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at TU Delft, as an assistant professor at the Leiden Computational Biology Center of the Leiden University Medical Center, and as an assistant professor in the Computer Graphics and Visualization group at TU Delft. His research focuses on visualization and visual analytics, in particular for high-dimensional data and biomedical applications such as single-cell and spatial omics. He leads Cytosplore, a visual analytics platform for single-cell data used by researchers around the world, and is involved in the development of ManiVault, an open-source framework for building visual analytics applications. His work received the Dirk Bartz Prize for Visual Computing in Medicine (2019), the VRVis Visual Computing Award (2023), and best paper and honorable mention awards at IEEE VIS, VCBM and PacificVis.</p>
